---
title: The Boulder
date: 2026-05-12
---

Cresting a ridge, the boulder rolls into the shallow gully below. My boulder. A range towers on the horizon, but then again, these hills once seemed impassable from a distance.

Grateful for the brief respite, I follow it down and drink from the creek in which it has settled.

Lungs and legs recovered, I place my hands on the stone and begin to push up the far side. The surface is rough and the pitch is steep, but my hands are calloused and my body is strong.

My boulder.
